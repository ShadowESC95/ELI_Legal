# Legal source connector standard

Every connector must declare: authority/owner; permitted use/licensing; jurisdictions; source types; retrieval timestamp; source/version identifier; effective/relevant date; freshness semantics; citation identity/pinpoint capabilities; treatment/current-law semantics; outage/failure behaviour; rate limits; and how a human can independently inspect the original source.

A connector result is not automatically a verified legal proposition. Citation existence, proposition support and current legal status are distinct checks.
