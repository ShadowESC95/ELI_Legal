# Open questions for counsel

The questions a qualified lawyer needs to answer before ELI Legal is sold or used on a real
matter. They are grouped by who has to ask. Each has a note of what the answer changes.

## For the provider

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Under what licence is the software offered, and what do the customer terms say about intended purpose, prohibited uses, liability, support and security updates? | Nothing can lawfully be distributed without one |
| P2 | Is the system high-risk under Annex III point 8(a) for each profile, or can Article 6(3) be relied on for any of them? | Decides the whole provider workload under the AI Act |
| P3 | Who is the "provider" in law: an individual, a company? Is an authorised representative needed? | Duties and liability attach to that person |
| P4 | What is the plan and timetable for the quality management system, Annex IV documentation, conformity assessment, declaration and registration? | Required before the high-risk obligations apply |
| P5 | Does offering it as an ELI v3 edition, or letting a customer rebrand it, change who the provider is (Article 25)? | Could shift duties to or from the customer |
| P6 | Does the Cyber Resilience Act apply, in which class, and what support period will be declared? | Reporting duties started in September 2026 |
| P7 | Is the Qt licence position satisfied by the installers as built, or is a commercial Qt licence the safer course? | Affects how installers may be packaged |
| P8 | Is the name free to use and register as a trade mark? | Before any marketing spend |
| P9 | What may the marketing say about verification, accuracy and compliance? | Misleading claims create liability |
| P10 | What insurance is needed? | |
| P11 | Are the jurisdiction packs, once validated by a local lawyer, something the provider warrants, or the deployer's responsibility? | Allocation of risk for wrong citation grammar or court hierarchy |
| P12 | Should the provider ever host the system for customers? If so, on what processor terms? | Changes the data-protection role entirely |

## For a deploying court, tribunal or arbitral body

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Does any rule, practice direction or judicial guidance govern use of AI by decision-makers here, and does this use fit it? | |
| D2 | Must the parties be told the tool is used? How, and when? | AI Act Article 26(11); fair procedures; in arbitration, the first procedural order |
| D3 | If the report raises an authority or point no party cited, what is the procedure for putting it to them? | The main fair-procedures risk |
| D4 | Is the report part of the court or tribunal file? Is it disclosable? How long is it kept? | Records, open justice, access requests |
| D5 | Is a fundamental-rights impact assessment required, and who signs it? | AI Act Article 27 |
| D6 | Which body supervises the data processing when the court acts judicially? | GDPR Article 55(3) |
| D7 | Who are the named reviewers, and what makes their review real rather than formal? | Human oversight; automated-decision case law |
| D8 | Does the public body's accessibility duty apply to the browser application? | A conformance statement will be needed |
| D9 | Which procurement route applies? | |

## For a deploying firm, chambers or in-house team

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Do our regulator's rules and guidance permit this use, and what must clients be told? | |
| F2 | What do the courts we appear in require: verification, disclosure, declarations? | Sanctions are real |
| F3 | Is privilege safe given where the data is stored and who has access? | |
| F4 | What is our lawful basis, including for special-category and criminal-offence data, and is our privacy notice adequate? | |
| F5 | Has a data-protection impact assessment been done? | Almost certainly required |
| F6 | Does our subscription to each legal database permit loading its content into this tool? Has the publisher confirmed in writing? | The commonest licence breach |
| F7 | Does the licence of the model we intend to run permit professional use? | |
| F8 | Does our professional-indemnity policy cover AI-assisted work? | |
| F9 | What is our retention period for matters and for the audit ledger? | |
| F10 | Who may be added to which matters, and how are conflicts and information barriers respected? | Matter membership is the control |
