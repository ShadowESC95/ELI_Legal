# Classification assessment (per deployment)

Classification follows the intended purpose of the actual deployment, not the software in general.

- Deployer, and the profile used (`adjudicator`, `neutral_facilitator`, `party_representative`):
- Who uses the output, and for what decision:
- Is it used by, or on behalf of, a judicial authority, or in a similar way in alternative dispute
  resolution, to research and interpret facts and the law and apply the law to concrete facts
  (EU AI Act Annex III point 8(a))? Reasoning:
- If a derogation is considered (Article 6(3)): which condition, why it is met, and why the
  output does not materially influence the decision. If relied on, the documentation and
  registration duties that follow:
- Conclusion, and what would change it (new users, new purpose, more autonomy):
- Roles: who is the provider, who is the deployer, and where that is agreed in writing:
- Assessed by (name, role, date); next review:
