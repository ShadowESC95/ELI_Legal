# Impact assessment (fundamental rights and data protection)

One working form for the questions the fundamental-rights impact assessment (EU AI Act Art. 27,
where it applies to the deployer), the data-protection impact assessment (GDPR Art. 35) and the
AI system impact assessment (ISO/IEC 42001 6.1.4) have in common. Split it if your organisation
keeps them separately.

- The process the system is used in, how often, and for how long:
- People affected: parties, witnesses, people named in documents, the decision-maker:
- Personal data processed, including any special-category or criminal-offence data; lawful basis:
- Harms considered: wrong law relied on; evidence missed; one-sided analysis; over-reliance on a
  confident report; disclosure of confidential or privileged material; delay; unequal effect on a
  party without representation:
- For each harm: the controls relied on (ids), and the residual risk:
- Human oversight: who reviews, their competence and time, how they can stop or disregard output:
- What happens when the system is unavailable or stopped:
- Complaints and correction: how an affected person can challenge reliance on the output:
- Consultation (data protection officer, affected groups) and outcome:
- Approved by (name, role, date); next review:
