ELI Legal

Governed legal case preparation: decision support for qualified legal
professionals. It prepares; a named, qualified person decides. It renders no
verdict, award or settlement terms, and it is not legal advice.

What this installer does
  - Puts the program in the folder you choose.
  - Adds a menu entry and the commands "eli-legal" and "eli-legal-gui".
  - Touches nothing else. It does not start a service, open a network port or
    contact any server.

Where your data is kept
  Matters, the audit ledger and its key are kept in your own user data folder,
  separate from the program:
    Linux    ~/.local/share/eli-legal
    Windows  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ELI Legal
    macOS    ~/Library/Application Support/ELI Legal
  Removing the program never removes that folder. Back it up, and keep a copy
  of the audit key somewhere else: without the key the ledger cannot be
  verified.

Before using it on a real matter
  Read docs/README.md, in particular the legal and compliance folder. This
  software has not been conformity-assessed, validated on real matters, or
  checked by a lawyer qualified in your jurisdiction.

Third-party software included with this program is listed, with its licences,
in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the installation folder.
